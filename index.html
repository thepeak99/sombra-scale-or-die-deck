Enter Sombra

Privacy built to endure the quantum age.

What Sombra is

Quantum-safe privacy, native to Solana.

Live · devnet

Sealed notes

Value lives in encrypted notes, sealed to the recipient with ML-KEM-768 (NIST FIPS 203) + ChaCha20-Poly1305. Harvested ciphertext stays sealed.

Live · devnet

Hash-based proofs

Ownership and validity are proven with RISC0 zkVM STARKs — hash-based, no trusted setup, and no pairing-based wrapper a quantum adversary could forge.

Live · devnet

Solana-native

SPL tokens in, SPL tokens out. Not a new chain — a privacy extension for the liquidity you already have.

Built for a world with quantum computers and AI-scale surveillance.

Precise about "post-quantum"

What's quantum-safe — and what isn’t yet.

Quantum-safe in Sombra

PQ
  • Note contents — ML-KEM-768 + ChaCha20-Poly1305. Harvested ciphertext stays sealed.
  • Ownership & spend proofs — hash-based STARKs over SHA-256 commitments.
  • No signature to spend — authority is knowledge of your key, proven in zero knowledge.
  • Service links — ML-DSA (FIPS 204) authentication, hybrid ML-KEM TLS.

Still classical — Solana layer

Ed25519
  • Settlement & consensus on Solana.
  • Transaction signing & fee payers.
  • Prover-node voting keys — rotated after every use to limit exposure.

Our rule: if the authorization path is Ed25519, we don’t call it post-quantum.

Compliance posture

Privacy with accountability — not a mixer.

Public edges

Deposits and withdrawals are public. Value entering and leaving the pool is always visible and auditable.

Scoped disclosure

V2 design

Read keys rotate weekly, so you can disclose a date range — never a forever key. Auditors scan and trial-decrypt, so nothing can be quietly left off a list.

Lineage by design

V2 design

Every note carries the origin of the deposit it descends from — derived by the program, never chosen by the user.

Association sets

Roadmap

Planned: screening at deposit, plus in-proof association-set membership (Privacy-Pools-style). Direction, not shipped.

Integration points

Onramp / offramp

The entry and exit points. Users arrive from, and leave to, ordinary SPL accounts.

Roadmap

Private staking via LSTs

Register an existing LST — JitoSOL, INF, bSOL, mSOL. Yield lives in the token's rate, not the note. No new protocol work.

Roadmap

Client SDK

Note building, encryption, proving, discovery and finalization behind one library. Today: WASM primitives + wallet extension.

Design

Fee sponsorship

Relayers pay gas so a user's own Solana key needn't appear. Swig smart wallets as fee-payer / session-key layer.

Call to action

What to prep for integration.

Token & LST teams

Make it shieldable

  • Tell us which SPL mints you want registered.
  • LSTs fit natively: yield in the rate, fixed note amounts.
  • Plan round denominations at the edges — odd amounts fingerprint deposits to withdrawals.
Wallets & dApps

Design the UX

  • Async, multi-step sends: pending → proving → attesting → final.
  • Onramp / offramp as entry and exit; those legs are public.
  • Local note scanning, and seed backup: shielded funds recover from the seed only.
DeFi & infra

Think in intents

  • Contracts never hold shielded notes — design for intent + public settlement.
  • Relayers / fee sponsors: help remove the funding-wallet link.
  • Talk to us now about the SDK surface you need.
